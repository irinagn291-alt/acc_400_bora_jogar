# Bora Jogar

## What it is

Bora Jogar helps organizers and players in community football manage squads, schedule matches, find courts, record results, and track seasons. It works offline for local planning and recordkeeping, and teams can exchange copies and review messages through manually shared files.

## Launch and onboarding

1. On a fresh install, the app opens directly to the “Matchday” tab, titled “Bora Jogar”. There is no welcome carousel, sign-in screen, or permission prompt. The five tabs are “Matchday”, “Squad”, “Courts”, “Season”, and “Team chat”. Quoted app copy in this brief is from the current English project localization.
2. The first screen shows “GET YOUR TEAM TOGETHER”, then “Less planning.” and “More playing.” on separate lines, followed by “Your squad, your court, your next match. All in one place, even offline.” Below “Create your team” it says “Have an invitation? Choose a file to open, or send a copy directly to someone. Changes stay on this device until you share again.” The three informational tiles read “Build a squad”, “Plan a match”, and “Track a season”.
3. “Create your team” opens “Create team”. Under “Your team”, enter “Team name” and keep or replace the “City” default, “São Paulo”. The form says “You are the organizer. Create matches, approve chat and invite players when you're ready.” “Create” saves the team, selects it, and returns to “Matchday”. “Cancel” closes the form.
4. To join from a copy instead, tap the profile icon labeled “Profile & privacy”, then “Open a shared file”. Select the organizer’s team copy in the system file picker. The imported team becomes the selected team, and “Squad” identifies the role as “Participant”.

## Screens

### “Matchday” — “Bora Jogar”

- The profile icon, labeled “Profile & privacy” for accessibility, opens settings. The “Team” picker appears when there is more than one team. Selecting a team changes the active team.
- With a team selected, the screen shows its name, city, player count, and the counters “Played”, “Players”, and “Scheduled”. “NEXT KICKOFF” shows the next future unfinished, uncancelled match involving that team, with the teams, kickoff, court, and a count followed by “going”. Tap the card to open “Match details”.
- Without a qualifying upcoming match, the card says “The next game starts here.” and “Choose a court, set kickoff and bring the squad.” Organizers can tap “Create match” there or use the plus icon labeled “Create match” beside “Match calendar”.
- “Match calendar” shows fixtures in reverse chronological order, with the teams, court, and kickoff time, final score, or “Cancelled”. Tap any row to open “Match details”. An empty calendar says “No matches yet.”
- “Create another team” opens “Create team” and selects the newly created team on return.

### “Create match”

- “Fixture” contains “Home team”, prefilled with the selected team, and “Opponent”. If opponents are saved, “Saved opponents” offers “Choose” and the saved names. “Kickoff” selects a date and time from the present onward and starts about one day ahead. “Minutes” starts at 60 and steps from 15 to 180 in increments of five. “Opponent confirmed” starts off.
- “Court & season” contains “Court”, initially “Choose”, with court and city names, and “Season”, which defaults to the latest season when one exists. “Friendly match” leaves the fixture outside a season.
- The form says “Court details are an offline directory. Contact the venue yourself to check access; creating a match does not reserve a court.” “Save” adds the fixture to the calendar, adds its teams to the chosen season if needed, and closes the form. “Cancel” closes it without saving.

### “Match details”

- Shows both teams, the score, kickoff date and time, and court. A cancelled fixture shows “Cancelled”. “Done” closes the sheet. An unavailable fixture shows “Match not found.”
- On an unfinished, uncancelled match, “Your attendance” offers “No response”, “I'm in”, “Can't play”, and “Maybe”. It says “Attendance syncs when the app opens. Unsynced responses stay on this device.” The selection saves locally. Participants exchange responses through team files.
- “Add to Calendar” opens the system event editor with the fixture title, kickoff, end time based on match duration, court, and the note “Community football organized with Bora Jogar.” The user can edit, save, or cancel in that editor. “Start kickoff countdown” starts a Live Activity for the match. “End countdown” ends it.
- “Lineup” lists players by shirt number and name. Organizers use the switches labeled “Selected” for accessibility to include or remove players. Participants see checkmarks for selected players. Players associated with a participant can show “Going”, “Unavailable”, or “No response”. An empty roster says “Add players in Squad.”
- On an uncancelled match, “Organizer protocol” lets the organizer change “Kickoff”, “Court”, and “Opponent confirmed”, adjust “Home goals” and “Away goals”, and toggle “Final score”. The form says “Only final protocols count toward standings. Player goals are recorded separately and must match the team score.”
- “Goal log” lists goal minutes, scorers, and assists. Deleted scorers appear as “Removed player”. The unlabeled minus icon removes an entry. “Scorer” starts at “Choose”. “Assist” starts at “None” and excludes the selected scorer. “Minute” runs from zero through the match duration. “Record goal” adds the entry and clears the scorer and assist selections.
- “Cancel match” opens “Cancel this match?” with a destructive “Cancel match” action. Confirming marks the fixture “Cancelled”, keeps it in the calendar, and ends its countdown.

### “Squad”

- With no team, the screen says “Create a team on Matchday first.” With a team, it shows the team name, city, and “Organizer” or “Participant”. The “Team” picker appears for multiple teams.
- Under “Invitations”, “Share a copy” opens the system share sheet. Organizers share a team snapshot. Participants share their queued responses and requests. “Request a place in the squad”, shown to participants, queues a membership request for the organizer. The section says “Choose a file to open, or send a copy directly to someone. Changes stay on this device until you share again.”
- “Players” lists shirt numbers, names, positions, and counts labeled “goals” and “assists” from final, uncancelled matches. Organizers can swipe to delete a player. An ellipsis menu on a player associated with a participant offers “Block player”.
- “Add player” opens a form titled “Add player” with “Name”, “Shirt number” from 1 through 99, and “Position” choices “Goalkeeper”, “Defense”, “Midfield”, and “Forward”. “Midfield” is the initial position. “Save” adds the player. “Cancel” closes the form.
- “Opponents” lists saved opponents, which can be removed by swiping. “Add opponent” opens “Add opponent” with “Name”, “Save”, and “Cancel”. The section says “Keep teams you know here, then select them when creating a match. Share invitations through the system sheet.”

### “Courts”

- Shows “A court for every game.” and “Offline directory · approximate map pins. Access, opening hours and availability must be confirmed with the venue.”
- “Search city or court” filters by city or court name. “Nearby” asks for location access when needed, then sorts matching courts by approximate distance and shows distances in kilometers. The status can read “Finding nearby courts…”, “Sorted by approximate distance.”, “Location is unavailable. Search by city instead.”, “Could not find your location. Search by city instead.”, or “Search by city instead.”
- “Map” shows or hides a map with markers named for the filtered courts. It says “Map tiles need a connection. The court list remains available offline.” Tap a row in “Directory” to open “Court details”.
- “Court details” shows the name, address if supplied, city, and “Approximate location”. “Open in Maps” opens the pin in Apple Maps. “Venue source”, available for bundled listings, opens the venue or municipal reference. The screen says “This is a reference listing, not a reservation service. Check the venue before traveling.” The navigation back control returns to “Courts”.
- “Add your own court” opens “Add court”. The fields are “Court name”, “City”, “Address”, “Latitude”, and “Longitude”. The form says “Coordinates are optional to obtain from Maps, but required to place your court accurately in this directory.” “Save” adds the court to the directory and the match picker. “Cancel” closes the form.

### “Season”

- With no team, the screen says “Create a team on Matchday first.” With a team, it shows “Every game counts.” and the team name. With no seasons, it says “Create a season to group fixtures and build a table from final scores.”
- The organizer’s plus icon is labeled “Create season” for accessibility. It opens “Create season” with “Season name” and “Teams are added to the table when fixtures are created in this season.” “Create” saves and selects the season. “Cancel” closes the form.
- The “Season” picker switches among saved seasons. Entering the tab selects the latest season. Counters show “Played”, “Scheduled”, and “Teams”.
- “Standings” is horizontally scrollable. Its columns are “Team”, “P”, “W”, “D”, “L”, “GF”, “GA”, “GD”, and “Pts”. The screen says “3 points for a win, 1 for a draw. Ties: goal difference, goals scored, team name. Cancelled matches do not count.”
- “Player statistics” lists “appearances”, “goals”, and “assists” from final, uncancelled fixtures in the selected season. Appearances come from the lineup, and goals and assists come from the goal log.

### “Team chat”

- Shows “A good game starts with respect.” and “Messages appear after organizer approval. No links, personal contacts or abusive content. Reports hide the author immediately on your device.” “Share a copy” opens the system share sheet when a team is selected. With no team, only the introductory section is shown and the share button has no effect.
- With a team, enter text in “Message for review” and tap the arrow labeled “Send for review” for accessibility. A successful submission clears the composer. An organizer’s message goes into local review. A participant’s message waits to be sent in a shared file.
- “Organizer review” shows pending messages, “Membership request” entries, and “Message report” entries. An empty queue says “Nothing waiting for review.” “Approve” publishes a message, adds a requesting participant to the squad if they are eligible, or removes a reported message. “Reject” dismisses the item without approving it.
- “Approved messages” shows author, date, and selectable text. “Report” immediately hides the author on this device and either removes the message for an organizer or queues a report for a participant. The alert says “Report saved for the organizer. This author is hidden on your device.” Organizers also have “Block player”, which blocks participation and removes the author’s messages from the team. Participants have “Hide author”, which hides that author on their device.
- A pending-outbox count is followed by “requests waiting to sync”.

### “Profile & privacy”

- “Profile” contains “Your name”. Edits save as typed, up to 45 characters. This is the display name used for chat and membership requests.
- “Open a shared file” opens the system file picker. “Share a copy” opens the system share sheet and requires a selected team. The section says “Choose a file to open, or send a copy directly to someone. Changes stay on this device until you share again.”
- Under “Reminders”, “Match reminders” opens a confirmation titled “Match reminders” with “Get a reminder the day before kickoff. Your match calendar works if you decline.” “Enable” requests system notification authorization. “Cancel” leaves reminders off. Turning the toggle off clears pending reminders. The section says “Local reminders for tomorrow's match and newly confirmed opponents. No real-time delivery is promised.”
- Under “Your data”, “Export device data” opens the system save picker. “Privacy & community rules” opens a sheet with the same title and repeats the file-sharing and chat rules quoted above. “Done” closes that sheet.
- “Unhide authors on this device” appears when the local hide list is populated and clears that list. “Delete profile & data” opens a confirmation with the same title and destructive action. Confirming clears local profile and team data, custom courts, and pending requests, restores the bundled courts, cancels pending reminders, and ends the countdown. The app then shows “Profile and device data deleted.”
- Settings shows “Free, without ads or purchases.” followed by “Designed for community football.” as one text block, and the version “1.3.2 · 73”. “Done” returns to “Matchday”.

### Widgets and kickoff countdown

- The Home Screen and Lock Screen widget is named “Bora Jogar”. Its description is “Team scores, your next kickoff and the season table, from your device.” The small Home Screen widget shows “Last result” or “Your next result starts on the pitch.” Other Home Screen sizes show the next fixture or “No upcoming match”. The large size adds “played”, “wins”, up to five standings rows, and “Updated” with a time. Lock Screen variants show kickoff timing or an empty state of “No upcoming match” or “—”, depending on size.
- The interactive widget’s “I'm in” button queues attendance locally and changes to “Saved locally”. Opening Bora Jogar applies the response. Tapping the widget opens “Matchday” and selects its team if available.
- The Live Activity shows the fixture, court, and countdown on the Lock Screen. On devices with Dynamic Island, it also appears there. Tapping it opens “Matchday”.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- “Squad” and “Season” say “Create a team on Matchday first.” Use “Create your team” or import a team through “Open a shared file”. The chat composer also requires a selected team. “Courts” works immediately.
- “Create” in “Create team” requires a nonblank team name of at most 45 characters and a nonblank city. “Save” in “Add player” or “Add opponent” requires a nonblank name of at most 45 characters. “Create” in “Create season” requires a nonblank name of at most 50 characters.
- “Save” in “Create match” requires two distinct, nonblank team names of at most 45 characters each and a selected “Court”. “Choose” leaves the court unset. “Opponent confirmed” and assigning a named season are optional.
- “Save” in “Add court” requires a nonblank court name, a city, latitude from −90 to 90, and longitude from −180 to 180. Decimal commas and decimal points are accepted. “Address” is optional. A court search with no matches simply leaves “Directory” empty. Clear or change the query.
- “Selected” is disabled for a fixture in which the selected squad is neither side. “Record goal” requires a scorer, a different player for any assist, and an unused goal in the squad’s score. Increase “Home goals” or “Away goals” for the squad before logging another goal. The squad’s score cannot be lowered below its recorded goal count until entries are removed. Only “Final score” fixtures contribute to the table, and “Cancelled” fixtures stay visible in the calendar while dropping out of standings and statistics.
- “Start kickoff countdown” can show “Start the countdown within 8 hours of kickoff.” Start it during the roughly eight-hour window before a future kickoff. If it says “Live Activities are disabled in Settings.”, enable them in system Settings. Starting a countdown replaces the previous one. Finishing or cancelling the match ends it.
- “Send for review” is disabled for whitespace-only text or text over 500 characters. “Add a profile name first.” means to fill in “Your name”; the same requirement applies to “Request a place in the squad”. “Links and contact details are not allowed in chat.” means to remove the link or contact detail. “Your participation is blocked by the organizer.” means the team’s block prevents sending. An organizer’s own submission still says “Pending organizer review.” and must be approved.
- “Queued for organizer review. Open sync to send.” and “requests waiting to sync” refer to the local queue. Use “Share a copy” on the participant’s device, then “Open a shared file” on the organizer’s device. After the organizer reviews items, they share a fresh team copy for participants to open. The queue can remain visible after sharing because sending a copy does not clear it. The organizer’s device skips previously handled requests, and opening an older or already imported copy can have no visible effect. A newer eligible organizer copy replaces that imported team snapshot. A participant’s “No response” saves locally, while the organizer’s import accepts affirmative, negative, and maybe responses without applying that reset.
- New teams, players, opponents, courts, fixtures, and seasons are added as they are saved. The season table gains teams as fixtures are created, even before any final scores. Returning to “Season” selects the most recent season. “Nothing waiting for review.” clears when a submission arrives. “Approved messages” can be empty until approval.
- If location is declined or unavailable, use “Search city or court”. If notifications are declined, “Notifications are disabled. You can enable them in Settings.” appears. The match calendar remains usable. Reminders are for future, unfinished, uncancelled fixtures. The day-before alert can only be scheduled when kickoff is still more than a day away.
- App feedback generally appears under “Bora Jogar” with “OK”. Local storage failures can show “Could not save on this device.” followed by a system error. On launch the app may show “Recovered the last local backup.” or “Local data could not be read. Keep a copy before deleting the app.” File and system failures can also display system-provided error text.

## Permissions

- **Location while using the app.** Requested the first time the user taps “Nearby” in “Courts” if access has not been decided. Exact English usage description: “Sort the bundled court directory by your approximate distance. You can also search by city.”
- **Notifications.** Requested after the user turns on “Match reminders” and taps “Enable”. The app’s explanation is “Get a reminder the day before kickoff. Your match calendar works if you decline.” The subsequent system notification prompt has no app-defined usage-description string. Notifications can be titled “Match tomorrow” or “Opponent confirmed”.
- **Calendar.** “Add to Calendar” opens the system event editor without an app-initiated calendar access request. The configured English descriptions are “Send a team fixture to your calendar when you choose Add to Calendar.” and “Save the football fixture you choose without reading your other calendar events.”
- **Live Activities.** “Start kickoff countdown” depends on the system’s Live Activities setting. The app has no separate permission prompt or usage-description string for it.

## Absent

Login or accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, an account deletion flow, and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are absent. User-generated content is present, including team and player entries, fixtures, custom courts, and moderated team messages. “Delete profile & data” deletes the local profile and device data.

## Data and support

Team and profile data, chat drafts submitted for review, and attendance responses are kept on the device until the user shares a copy. Sharing uses the system share sheet, and recipients open copies manually. Organizers share team snapshots with approved visible messages. Participants share queued attendance responses, membership requests, messages, and reports. “Export device data” creates a separate copy chosen by the user. Local deletion does not recall copies already shared or events saved to Calendar.

There is no on-screen Contact or Support control. “Venue source” and “Open in Maps” are venue reference and navigation controls.

None.

## Platform

Runs on iPhone and iPad with a minimum of iOS 17.2. iPhone supports upright portrait and both landscape orientations. iPad also supports upside-down portrait. The interface is dark. The app supports English, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French, Spanish, and Italian, with system formatting for dates and times. There is no in-app region selector or region gate. The same six Brazilian listings are bundled across regions, “São Paulo” is the initial team city, and users can add courts elsewhere. Location changes directory ordering when “Nearby” is used.
