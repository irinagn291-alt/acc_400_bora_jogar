Bora Jogar helps you run a community soccer team. Build your roster, save opponents, and schedule matches with a kickoff time and location. Set your lineup, record final scores, and log goals and assists. Follow the standings and see each player's appearances and contributions over the season.

Share a copy of your team so players can view matches and lineups, respond with their availability, request a roster spot, and submit chat messages. Participants send responses back in a file, and the organizer reviews requests and approves messages for team chat.

Search the built-in directory of Brazilian soccer venues offline or add your own court. Open a venue in Apple Maps, add matches to your calendar, or use optional local reminders and a kickoff countdown. Widgets show match and season information, and the medium widget lets you RSVP for the next match.

Core tools use data stored on your device and work without an account.
