Bora Jogar helps you organise community football. Build your squad, save regular opponents and schedule fixtures with a kick-off time and venue. Select your line-up, record final scores and log goals and assists. Follow appearances, player contributions and league standings throughout the season.

Share a team copy so players can see fixtures and line-ups, respond with their availability, request a place in the squad and submit messages. Participants send their responses back in a file, and the organiser reviews requests and approves messages for team chat.

Search the built-in directory of Brazilian football venues offline or add your own court. Open a venue in Apple Maps, add a fixture to your calendar, or use optional local reminders and a kick-off countdown. Widgets show match and season information, and the medium widget lets you respond to the next fixture.

Core tools work with data saved on your device and need no account.
